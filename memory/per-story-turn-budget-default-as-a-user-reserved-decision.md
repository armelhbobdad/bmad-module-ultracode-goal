# Per-story turn budget default as a user-reserved decision

`max_turns_per_story` defaults to 25 in three mirrors (`skills/ultracode-goal/customize.toml`, `DEFAULT_MAX_TURNS`/`ULTRACODE_MAX_TURNS` in `skills/ultracode-goal/scripts/hooks/budget_stop.py`, and [docs/architecture.md](../docs/architecture.md)), unchanged since the initial commit, and the user reserved the question in the 2026-08-03 handoff prompt ("Two decisions that are mine, not yours: the per-story turn budget (default 25 vs raising it) ... Ask; don't pick.") and settled it the same session by selecting "Keep 25, change nothing" over "Split into two knobs" and "Raise the default". The number's real work is the story-sizing trigger, not the runaway bound: `execute.md` runs `story_sizing.py --turn-ceiling {workflow.max_turns_per_story}` (a row is implausible once `load >= ceiling / 2`), `preflight.md` derives the worst-case envelope from it, and [docs/troubleshooting.md](../docs/troubleshooting.md) advises re-scoping or splitting over raising the budget. The evidence put to the user was 67 `.budget-*.json` files across GMC runs with turns per story at max 9, median 2; an earlier session's figure of roughly 4 to 222 turns with false-positive escalations at 25 was the pre-ruling case for raising and did not carry, and this repo's own gitignored `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/` holds four budget files over the ceiling (26, 27, 28 and 78 turns), so "never approached" is not a standing fact. The default is settled: do not change it or any mirror, and do not re-propose raising or splitting it, without new budget evidence and without asking; if a split ever resurfaces, the honest framing is that one number does two jobs that want different values, not that 25 is wrong.
